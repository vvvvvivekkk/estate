Property Overview

A residence conceived not as a structure, but as a way of living with light, space and view.

From an open plot to a finished architectural work, the home unfolds as a single, continuous gesture — each space leading quietly into the next. Every threshold has been considered; nothing is incidental.

01

Architecture

A disciplined composition of form, structure and material — where the building and its light are inseparable.

  • Structural Form

    Clean, deliberate geometry that lets the architecture speak without ornament.

  • Materials

    A considered, warm-neutral material palette, selected for permanence and restraint.

  • Glass Facade

    Expansive glazing dissolves the line between the interior and the world beyond it.

  • Natural Light

    Daylight is treated as a material — drawn deep into the plan and shaped through the day.

Indoor and outdoor are held in a single continuous relationship, so the residence feels open to its surroundings while remaining entirely private.

02

The Residence

Space, openness and privacy in equal measure.

The home is organised around generous volumes and uninterrupted sightlines. Rooms flow into one another with an ease that feels effortless, yet each retains its own sense of quiet and seclusion.

Light moves through the plan from morning to evening, and the relationship between every area — public and private, open and intimate — has been calibrated for the rhythm of everyday life.

03

Living

An open-plan living environment framed by glass and sky.

  • Open Plan

    A single, fluid volume for gathering, dining and pause — generous and unhurried.

  • Glass Facade

    Full-height glazing brings the outside in and sets the living space against the horizon.

  • Natural Light

    Soft, directional daylight shifts across the room throughout the day.

  • Skyline Views

    The city and sky become a quiet, ever-present backdrop to daily life.

04 — Private Retreat The master bedroom is a space of calm — private, serene and composed around rest.

Withdrawn from the more open areas of the home, the retreat is deliberately quiet: soft light, generous proportion, and an uninterrupted view held in reserve for the end of the day.

05

Entertainment

A games room designed for gathering and play.

The entertainment level extends the character of the home into something more relaxed and social — a dedicated space to host, unwind and enjoy, without compromising the serenity of the residence above.

06 — The View The residence, its architecture and the skyline beyond are composed as one continuous horizon.

Framed by glass at every turn, the surrounding environment is never incidental — it is part of the architecture itself, drawn into every room and held in view.
